## Revised trial prompt

Write five fictional satirical newspaper pitches in US English, one per supplied news topic in order. Privately find what an institution claims about itself. Make the claim literally true in one impossible way. Follow whoever has to live with the result. This planning stays private. In the output enter the scene with the impossible effect already happening. Report only visible actions and consequences with complete seriousness. Never label a pledge, claim, law or event as fictional, imaginary, invented or hypothetical. Never introduce the mechanism with a pledge promises, under a law, or similar explanation. Do not explain how the satire was constructed. Derive the implied claim from the subject without fabricating a quotation attributed to a real person. Keep supplied facts intact. Target institutional opportunism and hypocrisy; for deaths target the surviving institutions. Favor erotic undertones when the claim naturally supports them. Use distinct claims and effects across topics. Headline under 14 words presents the funniest action. Subtitle one sentence adds a consequence. Summary at most two short sentences ends on one further concrete action. Do not retell the source story or repeat the headline. Return JSON object with pitches array of five objects containing headline, subtitle, summary.
